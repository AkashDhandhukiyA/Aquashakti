## Standard Engineering Features Across All Models

Every commercial RO unit manufactured in our Vapi facility is built to rigorous industrial standards:

- **Stainless Steel 304 Grade Frame**: Anti-corrosive, skid-mounted frame that will never rust, even in humid environments.
- **Imported High-Rejection Membranes**: Trusted thin-film composite membranes from global leaders (Filmtec, Hydranautics, Toray).
- **FRP & SS Pressure Vessels**: High burst-pressure safety factor.
- **Vertical Multi-Stage High Pressure Pump**: Smooth, quiet vertical pump (CRI / CNP / Grundfos) designed for 24/7 continuous duty.
- **Comprehensive Instrumentation**:
  - Dual Flow Meters (Rota Meters) measuring permeate (pure) and reject flow rates.
  - High Pressure and Low Pressure Gauges (glycerin-filled).
  - Low Pressure Switch (LPS) protecting pumps against dry-run damage.
  - High Pressure Switch (HPS) stopping the unit when storage tanks are full.
- **Automated Flush System**: Periodically flushes high-velocity raw water across membrane surfaces to remove concentrated scaling salts.
- **Integrated Control Panel**: Complete with power on/off switches, indicator lamps, voltmeter, and digital level controller.
- **Tertiary UV Disinfection Chamber**: Stainless steel 304 chamber with high-output germicidal ultraviolet lamp destroying bacteria and viruses.
