## 1. Raw Water Analysis: The Foundation of Design

Before selecting pumps or membrane housings, a comprehensive chemical analysis of the raw feed water is essential. The key parameters required include:

- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)**: Determines the osmotic pressure required and pump head sizing.
- **Silt Density Index (SDI)**: Must be $< 3.0$ (maximum $< 5.0$) before entering RO membranes to avoid particulate fouling.
- **Total Hardness (Calcium & Magnesium)**: Determines antiscalant chemical dosing requirements.
- **Silica ($SiO_2$) Levels**: High silica can lead to irreversible membrane scale if recovery exceeds saturation points.
- **Iron, Manganese, and Free Chlorine**: Chlorine must be strictly 0 ppm (using SMBS dosing or Activated Carbon) to prevent polyamide membrane oxidation.

## 2. Pre-Treatment Train Architecture

Membranes are high-precision components that require dedicated pre-treatment stages:

```
[Raw Water Tank] ──> [Feed Pump] ──> [Dual Media Filter (Sand/Anthracite)] 
                 ──> [Activated Carbon Filter] ──> [Antiscalant Dosing] 
                 ──> [5 Micron Micron Cartridge Filter] ──> [High Pressure Vertical Pump] 
                 ──> [RO Membrane Pressure Vessels] ──> [Permeate Water Tank]
```

### Pre-treatment Components supplied by Aqua Shakti:
1. **Multi-Grade Sand Filter**: Traps suspended particles down to 20–30 microns.
2. **Activated Carbon Filter (ACF)**: High iodine value virgin carbon bed to adsorb organic matter, free chlorine, odor, and color.
3. **Chemical Dosing System**: Electronic metering dosing pumps injecting proprietary Antiscalant chemicals to prevent calcium carbonate and sulphate precipitation.
4. **Micron Cartridge Housing**: Precision 5-micron spun polypropylene filters serving as the final security barrier before the high-pressure stage.

## 3. High-Pressure Pumps & Membrane Configuration

Operating pressures range from **12 bar to 22 bar** for brackish borewell water, and up to **60–70 bar** for seawater desalination plants.

- **Vertical Multi-Stage Centrifugal Pumps**: Manufactured in SS 304 / SS 316 for chemical resilience and quiet, non-vibrating continuous operation.
- **Membrane Selection**: Thin-film composite (TFC) polyamide membranes (8-inch diameter for industrial systems; 4-inch for commercial systems) delivering up to 99.5% salt rejection.
- **FRP & SS Pressure Vessels**: Multi-element pressure vessels designed for 300 PSI to 1000 PSI operating limits with secure Victaulic coupling ports.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators to Monitor

A well-instrumented Aqua Shakti RO plant incorporates telemetry for real-time monitoring:

| Metric | Normal Range | Action If Out of Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Feed TDS** | 500 – 4000 ppm | Adjust recovery / anti-scalant dosing |
| **Permeate TDS** | 10 – 80 ppm | Check membrane O-ring seals or degradation |
| **Recovery Rate** | 60% – 75% | Reduce recovery if scaling risk increases |
| **Differential Pressure ($\Delta P$)** | $< 1.5$ bar per stage | Trigger CIP (Clean-In-Place) chemical wash |
| **Permeate Flow (Rota Meter)** | Rated flow $\pm 10\%$ | Check pump RPM, inlet pressure, temperature |

---

## 5. CIP (Clean-In-Place) Maintenance Protocols

Even with optimal pre-treatment, membrane surfaces gradually accumulate biofouling or inorganic scaling. An automated or semi-automated CIP skid allows operators to circulate specialized acid cleaners (for scaling removal) and alkaline cleaners (for organic removal) without removing membranes from their vessels.
